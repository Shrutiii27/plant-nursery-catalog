Plant Care Tips

💧 Watering

Water in the morning. Check the soil with your finger first. If the top inch is dry, it is time to water.

☀️ Sunlight

Most plants need 4 to 6 hours of light. Turn the pot a little every week so it grows evenly.

🪴 Soil and Pots

Use a pot with drain holes. Mix garden soil with compost for healthy roots.
